RESEARCH PILOT · EVIDENCE PENDING
Open-source AI × standards

Make documented participation observable.

A small, evidence-first research project asking what forms of participation by open-source AI projects in formal standardisation can be established from public records—and what those records leave unknown.

Read the method ↓Read related research ↗
Research integrity notice: This is a scaffold, not a completed empirical study. No project-to-standards participation relationship is currently presented as verified. Findings will be published only after manual source audit.

01 · Research question

What forms of open-source AI participation in formal standardisation can be documented from public evidence?

02 · Evidence standard

Every relationship requires a source URL, exact supporting passage, access date, coding decision and limitation.

03 · Analytical restraint

A few verified records can illustrate a method; they cannot support broad ecosystem or brokerage claims.

Research workflow

1. DefineSet the sample, observation period and inclusion rules.
2. CodeSeparate membership, contribution, consultation, alignment and contextual mentions.
3. VerifyAudit 5–8 candidate relationships against primary or official sources.
4. DescribePublish a small network only after validation; no causal claims.

Research foundations

Heß, Buggenhagen & Blind (2025). The Impact of Standard-Setting Knowledge Networks on Firm Innovation. DOI ↗

Studies formal standard-setting co-authorship networks and firm patent outcomes. It motivates a possible extension; it does not establish that the same relationships hold in open-source AI.

Blind & Kromer (2025). The Role of Research and Other Inputs for the Development and Impacts of Standards. DOI ↗

Provides a foundation for treating standards as a channel for knowledge and technology transfer.

TU Berlin: Open Source Software & Standardization ↗

# Working Research Protocol

## Core question
What forms of participation by open-source AI projects in formal standardisation can be documented from public evidence?

## Supporting questions
1. Which projects have verifiable evidence of membership, contributions, consultation responses, liaison activity, or other defined participation?
2. What documented pathways connect open-source AI development with standards and formal governance?
3. Which relationships remain unestablished after a documented search, and why?

## Method
1. Predefine eligible projects, venues, observation period, and inclusion/exclusion rules.
2. Search primary sources first: official rosters, committee pages, minutes, submissions, contribution records, and governance pages.
3. Record the claim, exact source passage/locator, URL, access date, relationship type, and caveats.
4. Apply the codebook consistently and keep participation separate from alignment and contextual mention.
5. Independently audit an initial 5–8 candidate relationships before expanding the sample.
6. Keep an audit trail for accepted, rejected, and unresolved claims.
7. Report only descriptive statistics supported by the coverage and completeness of the dataset.

## Reporting boundaries
The demo records are not representative and do not support ecosystem-wide estimates, causal claims, or brokerage claims. Establish literature-gap claims through a documented literature review. Report search scope, date range, exclusions, missingness, limitations, and dataset version.
